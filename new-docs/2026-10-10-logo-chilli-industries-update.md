## Logo

The delivered logo pack (`Primary Logo.jpg`, `Secondary Logo.png`, `Sub-Mark Logo.png`,
`Favicon Logo.jpg`) is a new design — a circle+leaf+"MR" monogram with "Malabar Reserve"
wordmark and tagline "For the Love of Spice" — replacing the earlier pepper-berry icon
lockup. All four files ship flattened with an opaque cream background baked in (confirmed
by reading corner pixel alpha — `A:255` everywhere), the same situation as the very first
logo pack back in June, so the same chroma-key approach was reused:

1. `Favicon Logo.jpg` (the icon mark alone) and `Sub-Mark Logo.png` (the wordmark alone, no
   icon) were each chroma-keyed to remove the uniform cream background, then auto-cropped to
   their actual content bounds.
   - A ~1px border artifact from GDI+ bicubic resizing was initially throwing off the
     auto-crop bounding box (it was reporting near-full-canvas bounds); fixed by insetting
     the scan a few pixels from each edge before computing the box.
2. The cropped icon and wordmark were composed side-by-side into one horizontal lockup
   (`logo-navbar-dark.png`) — this mirrors the "icon + wordmark side-by-side" layout the
   previous logo era settled on in `color-and-logo-update.md`, but as a single flattened
   image instead of icon-image + live coded text.
3. A light/dark-background variant (`logo-navbar-light.png`) was generated by recoloring:
   green ink → white, gold ink (`R - B > 30` heuristic) left unchanged. Verified by
   compositing onto a forest-green test swatch before committing to it.
4. `Logo.tsx` now renders `logo-navbar-dark.png` for `variant="dark"` (Navbar once
   scrolled/on non-home pages, used everywhere except the homepage hero state) and
   `logo-navbar-light.png` for `variant="light"` (homepage hero Navbar, Footer) — a straight
   image swap, no more icon+live-text hybrid.
5. Favicons (`favicon-32.png`, `favicon-192.png`) were regenerated from the new transparent
   icon mark (`logo-icon-mark.png`).
6. `Primary Logo.jpg` (full lockup incl. tagline) and `Sub-Mark Logo.png` (original,
   un-cropped) were copied into `photos-to-use/` as `logo-primary-full.jpg` and
   `logo-submark-wordmark.png` — not wired into any page, kept for potential future use
   (e.g. a larger brand moment on a future page).

**Not changed:** the "Our Core Values" section heading/subtitle itself, the 4-icon feature
